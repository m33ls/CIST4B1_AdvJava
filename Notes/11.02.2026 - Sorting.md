

## Simplification in Big O

$$O(n^2+n) = O(n^2) $$
$$\lim_{n\to\infty} n^2 + n = \lim_{n\to\infty} n^2$$

* Big O notation represent time complexity growth as n approaches infinity, which means the simplification of two functions functions like a limit

$$c \times O(f(n)) = O(f(n))$$
$$c + O(f(n)) = O(f(n))$$
$$g(n) \times O(f(n)) = O(g(n) \times f(n))$$
$$g(n) + O(f(n)) = O(g(n) + f(n))$$

## Selection Sort
* Like sorting a deck of playing cards one by one
* The algorithm maintains two part of the list: the sorted portion and the unsorted portion
* Scan unsorted for smallest, swap into first element of the sorted portion

## Insertion Sort
* Very closely related to Selection Sort
    * Maintins unsorted and sorted
* Rather than scanning unsorted section for the smallest and shuffling it forward, the first unsorted element is taken and placed in its order in the sorted array
